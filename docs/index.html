SoundCloud client for Android and iOS

Music, your way.

An open source SoundCloud client with Material 3 Expressive at its core. Lyrics with Genius notes, a waveform you can feel, and your Telegram music in the same library.

Home

Player

Artwork in expressive shapes pulses to the beat, progress wraps around it, and timed comments show up at their second.

  • graphic_eqWaveform, wavy or classic seek bar

Lyrics

Synced, and explained.

Lines from LRCLIB stretch to fit the SoundCloud version, and Genius notes sit on the lines worth a story.

  • timerAuto stretch and per-track offset
  • lightbulbGenius notes in place
  • ios_shareLyric cards to share
Search

Only know one line?

Type the words you remember. Resona finds the song through Genius and plays it from SoundCloud in one tap.

  • manage_searchSearch by a lyric line
  • linkSoundCloud links with timestamps
Telegram

Your Telegram music, right here.

Pick a chat or a channel. Resona scans it for audio, finds what is on SoundCloud, and saves the rest for offline.

  • joinMatch first or download all
  • library_musicOne library with SoundCloud and local files
  • cloud_offPlays offline from the start
